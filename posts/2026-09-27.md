# X draft for 2026-09-27

Post as a thread. Character counts use X's weighting (URLs = 23).

## 1/6 (244 chars)

How many people are living with a brain-computer interface implant? Published counts run from 50 to 254.

So I built a census where every person counted has a source. As of Sep 27, 2026: at least 103, across 7 programs.

https://jikjii.github.io/bci-census/

## 2/6 (213 chars)

Why the counts disagree: they mix permanent implants, temporary ones placed during surgery, and trial enrollment targets. Some leave out China. Few subtract devices that were removed. The census keeps these apart.

## 4/6 (203 chars)

How a number gets in: registry records and papers (tier A), company statements (B) or credible press (C). Aggregator totals are shown but never counted. Trial targets never count, only actual enrollment.

## 5/6 (199 chars)

Every Monday the census re-pulls ClinicalTrials.gov (64 implanted BCI trials so far), FDA decisions and SEC Form D filings, and this account posts what changed. Code and data: https://github.com/jikjii/bci-census
